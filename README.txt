AIF-C01 Study Lab
=================
Open index.html in your browser (double-click it, or drag it onto Chrome / Safari / Edge).
No install. No internet required after the files are on this computer.

Study path: Domain 1 → each topic page (read + quiz) → Domains 2–5 → mixed quizzes → 5 mocks.
Progress (plan ticks, quiz scores, mock history) stays in this browser only.

To rebuild topic pages after editing js/_build/*.py:
  python3 js/_build/build.py
