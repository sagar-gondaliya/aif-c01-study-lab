Lesson 8 — Guardrails and responsible AI (from your notes)

Your AIP notes were strongest here. This is the Practitioner cut. Domain 4 + part of 5.

The six features

Bias, fairness, inclusivity, robustness, safety, veracity. Add sustainability: do not default to the largest FM for a FAQ.

Datasets should be inclusive, diverse, curated, balanced. Legal risks: IP claims, biased output, lost trust, user harm, fluent lies.

Guardrail types (memorise)

  • Content filters — hate, insults, sexual, violence, misconduct
  • Denied topics — “no legal / medical advice”
  • Word / regex — internal code names
  • Sensitive information — PII/PHI redact or block
  • Contextual grounding — flag answers not in the retrieved docs
  • Automated reasoning — logical / policy checks

Stem: “prevent the model from giving legal advice” or “answers only from approved sources.”
Answer: Guardrails (denied topic and/or grounding), not “add a sentence to the prompt.”
Need to detect but not block? Monitoring mode, or Clarify on a batch.
