Lesson 2 — AWS AI services you must recognise

These are the easiest points on the exam if you learn one job per name. Cover the left column and say the service out loud.

AWS style: “We have call recordings and want text, then sentiment.” Answer: Transcribe + Comprehend. Not Polly (that is the opposite direction). Not Rekognition (that is images).

Personalisation

Amazon Personalize is the “Amazon.com recommendations” service. Real-time product or content ranking. It is not an LLM and not a chatbot.

Pairs that always appear together

  • Audio in → Transcribe. Text out loud → Polly. Never swap them.
  • PDF form fields → Textract. “What objects are in this photo?” → Rekognition.
  • Sentiment of a sentence → Comprehend. Translation → Translate.
  • Structured chatbot with slots → Lex. Open-ended company Q&A → Q / Knowledge Bases.

From your extra notes (AIP-C01) — keep only this much
